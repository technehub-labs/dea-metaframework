# Case Study — Telecom

The foundation matrix populated for a telecom operator. The cell address is
identical to any other industry; only the cell content changes.

| Domain \ Stage | Conceive | Design | Build | Activate | Operate | Improve | Retire |
|----------------|----------|--------|-------|----------|---------|---------|--------|
| **Governance & Existence** | Reg. mandate (TRA) | Controls design (SOX) | Compliance build (DPI) | Audit enforce | Lawful intercept | Risk review | Policy repeal |
| **Supply & Resources** | Spectrum vision (RAN) | Core arch. (EPC/5GC) | Equip. install | Network integ. | NMS monitoring | KPI utilization (erlang) | Equip. retire |
| **People & Organization** | Field force plan | NOC/org design | Engineer training | Crew dispatch | Performance (OKR) | Engagement | Redeploy |
| **Customer & Demand** | Subscriber need | Tariff plans | SIM provisioning | Network attach (HLR/HSS) | Customer care (CRM) | Churn scoring (ARPU) | Number port (MNP) |
| **Product & Offering** | Service roadmap (5G) | Service catalog (BSS) | Bundle config | Commercial launch | Catalog lifecycle (OSS) | Service uptake | Plan sunset |
| **Operations & Delivery** | Traffic forecast | Network design | Circuit prov. | Site cut-over | NOC operations (24/7) | Fault mgm (TT) | Site decom. |
| **Finance & Value** | Investment case | Tariff model (regulator) | Funding approval | Billing start (mediation) | Revenue recog. (ARPU) | Margin by plan (EBITDA) | Asset impair. |

## Telecom Patterns

- The **Customer × Activate** cell — network attach via HLR/HSS — is the
  telco's highest-risk handoff; the matrix isolates it for investment.
- The **Finance × Operate** cell — mediation and rating — runs continuously;
  the matrix shows it as the revenue engine, not a back-office afterthought.

## Worked Example — Subscriber Lifecycle (Telco)

| Stage | Cell | What happens |
|-------|------|-------------|
| Conceive | Customer × Conceive | Subscriber need identified (market research, lead gen) |
| Design | Customer × Design | Tariff plans designed, SIM form factor decided |
| Build | Customer × Build | SIM provisioned, account created, billing accounts opened |
| Activate | Customer × Activate | HLR/HSS registration, network attach, first call/data |
| Operate | Customer × Operate | Customer care via CRM, usage collection, ARPU tracking |
| Improve | Customer × Improve | Churn scoring, upsell, NPS survey |
| Retire | Customer × Retire | Number port-out (MNP), account closure, billing settlement |

Same 7 stages; the cell address is uniform across industries. Only the
content changes.
