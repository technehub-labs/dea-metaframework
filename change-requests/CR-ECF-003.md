CR-ECF-003 — ECF Domain Grounding

Status: Proposed
Type: Semantic Grounding
Implements: CR-ECF-001
Depends On: CR-ECF-001, CR-ECF-002

1. Change Request

Establish a formal grounding record for the seven ECF Domains.

The current seven Domains shall be retained provisionally:

1. Governance & Existence
2. Supply & Resources
3. People & Organization
4. Customer & Demand
5. Product & Offering
6. Operations & Delivery
7. Finance & Value

The current REPORT already derives these domains from the enterprise grounding axiom and provides definitions for each. (GitHub⁠)

This CR does not rename or restructure the domains without evidence.

2. Grounding Record

Each Domain shall have:

Domain
    ↓
Axiom Grounding
    ↓
Semantic Definition
    ↓
Included Concerns
    ↓
Excluded Concerns
    ↓
Adjacent Domains
    ↓
Boundary Rules
    ↓
Evidence / Rationale

3. Compound Domain Audit

The following compound names shall receive explicit boundary treatment:

Governance + Existence
Supply + Resources
People + Organization
Customer + Demand
Product + Offering
Operations + Delivery
Finance + Value

The purpose is to determine whether each compound is:

* semantically necessary;
* a deliberate aggregation;
* merely a naming convenience;
* or requires future decomposition.

No decomposition shall be introduced merely because the name contains two nouns.

4. Domain Orthogonality

The seven Domains shall remain independent of the seven Stages.

Knowledge of Domain shall not determine Stage.

Knowledge of Stage shall not determine Domain.

This preserves the ECF’s existing orthogonality principle. (GitHub⁠)

5. Domain Completeness

The grounding exercise shall test whether the seven domains collectively cover the enterprise definition without:

* gaps;
* uncontrolled overlap;
* hidden assumptions;
* imported framework categories.

6. Acceptance Criteria

* [ ]	All seven domains have formal grounding records.
* [ ]	Compound-domain boundaries are documented.
* [ ]	Inclusion/exclusion rules exist.
* [ ]	Adjacent-domain relationships are documented.
* [ ]	Domain orthogonality is demonstrated.
* [ ]	No domain is changed without explicit evidence and governance.
