Axiom

Where the matrix comes from

A single sentence — "an enterprise is any bounded entity that persists by exchanging value with its environment" — generates seven domains. A universal lifecycle of seven stages partitions the work of any object over time. The framework is not imported from eTOM, ITIL, or COBIT; it is derived from the definition of an enterprise itself.

How to navigate

Browse the matrix

Pick a scenario below — Foundation, Telecom, or Digital Services — and click any cell to inspect its objects, capabilities, and handoffs. The matrix is the same shape across industries; only the cell content changes. Same skeleton, same rigor, every enterprise.
